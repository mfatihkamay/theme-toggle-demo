What it does

  • Three options: Light, Dark, System.
  • System follows the phone/computer theme and updates live when it changes.
  • Choice saved in the browser (also synced across open tabs).
  • Phone, tablet and desktop: 44px tap targets, keyboard: Tab, then arrow keys, Space/Enter.
  • Any number of toggles on one page. Just add <span data-theme-toggle></span>.
